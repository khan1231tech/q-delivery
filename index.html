Privacy Policy

Open the full Privacy Policy in your browser ↗

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Q-Delivery Malakand respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, used and shared when you use the Q-Delivery customer app, place an order, communicate with a restaurant or use customer support features.

1. Information We Collect

  • Name, email address and phone number provided for an account, order or customer profile.
  • If you sign in with Google, your name, email address and profile photo as provided by Google.
  • A device notification token (via Firebase Cloud Messaging) used to deliver order-status notifications.
  • Delivery address entered or confirmed by you when placing an order. It is used to deliver your order.
  • Device location, only when you tap “Use Current Location”, and the delivery pin you place on the checkout map. The delivery coordinates (latitude and longitude) are saved with your order.
  • The district you select (for example Malakand or Swat), used to show the restaurants and delivery services available in that district.
  • Order information such as restaurant, ordered items, total amount, payment method, order status and order time.
  • Messages and information you provide when using customer support or chat features.
  • Rider location shown to you through live delivery tracking, when it is available for your order.

2. How We Use Information

  • To create and manage your customer account.
  • To process, confirm and deliver your orders.
  • To show you the restaurants and delivery services available in your selected district.
  • To calculate the delivery fee from the distance between the restaurant and your delivery pin, and to show the rider’s progress while your order is delivered.
  • To communicate order status and delivery information.
  • To enable communication with restaurants when you use the restaurant chat feature.
  • To provide live or near-real-time delivery tracking when available.
  • To provide customer support and resolve order-related issues.
  • To maintain, improve and secure Q-Delivery services.

3. Data Storage

Q-Delivery uses Supabase services for application data, account information and order-related records.

Restaurant-submitted content such as promotional banners and combo/meal deals is stored using the same Supabase-based systems used for menu and order data, and is shown to customers only after Q-Delivery review and approval.

Some customer preferences, account-related settings and notification information may also be stored locally on your device. This includes your selected district (so the app remembers it the next time you open the app), your most recent delivery address and map coordinates, saved addresses, favourites, theme and language choice, and any promo code you have applied.

4. Location & District Information

Q-Delivery uses your device location only when you tap “Use Current Location” on the checkout map. Before the Android permission request appears, the app explains why location is needed. Opening the checkout screen does not read your location.

You can deny location permission and manually enter your delivery address instead.

Your location is read once for each tap, and is used to place the delivery pin and fill in your delivery address. Q-Delivery does not track your location continuously and does not use background location. No location watcher keeps running after you close checkout or confirm your order.

To turn map coordinates into a readable address, Q-Delivery sends the relevant coordinates to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim reverse-geocoding service (a third-party, non-advertising service used only for this purpose). Your full delivery address and a shortened version of it are then stored locally on your device so they can be reused the next time you order.

When live rider tracking is available for your order, the rider's position and your delivery pin may be sent to a road-routing service (OpenRouteService by HeiGIT, or the public OSRM service) solely to draw the route and estimate arrival time. Map tiles for the checkout and tracking maps are loaded from OpenFreeMap and OpenStreetMap, which can see your IP address and the map area being viewed.

Why we use location: your location or map pin, together with the address you enter, tells the restaurant and rider where to deliver your order, lets the app calculate the delivery fee from the distance between the restaurant and your delivery pin, and lets you follow the rider during delivery. The coordinates are saved with your order and kept on your device so they can be reused for your next order.

District: the first time you open the app you are asked to choose your district. The app uses it only to show the restaurants and delivery services available in that district, and sends it to our database only as a filter when the restaurant list is requested. The selected district is saved on your device so the app remembers it, and you can change it at any time by tapping the district name at the top of the home screen. It is not used to track you and is not shared for advertising.

5. Promotional Banners & Combo Deals

Restaurants may submit promotional banners and combo/meal deals for display in the app. Promotional banners are reviewed and approved by Q-Delivery before they are shown to any customer.

If you tap a promotional banner, you may be taken to the related restaurant, and any promo code associated with that banner may be applied automatically. That promo code is stored locally on your device (not on Q-Delivery's servers) until it is used or replaced by a different one.

Combo/meal deals are created and priced by the restaurant. When you order a combo deal, its details are included in your order in the same way as any other menu item, and are shared with the restaurant so it can prepare and fulfil your order.

6. Restaurant & Delivery Sharing

Information necessary to fulfill an order may be shared with the relevant restaurant and delivery personnel.

This may include your name, phone number, delivery address, order details and information necessary to complete the delivery.

Rider location information may be displayed to you through the live tracking feature while your delivery is in progress.

7. Chat & Customer Support

When you use the restaurant chat feature, messages you send may be shared with the relevant restaurant so that it can respond to your order-related communication.

Messages sent to Q-Delivery customer support may be stored and reviewed when necessary to provide support, investigate problems or resolve disputes.

8. Sharing of Information

Q-Delivery does not sell customer information.

Information may be shared only when reasonably necessary to provide the service, process an order, complete a delivery, provide customer support, maintain the service or comply with applicable legal requirements.

When you use the checkout map, coordinates you select or your device's current position may be sent to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service solely to convert them into a readable address, as described in Section 4. During live rider tracking, coordinates may also be sent to a routing service (OpenRouteService or OSRM), as described in Section 4.

9. Sign-In, Notifications & Third-Party Services

If you choose "Continue with Google" to sign in, Google provides Q-Delivery with your name, email address and profile photo (if available) so your account and order history can be linked to you across devices. You may instead choose "Continue as Guest", in which case no Google account information is collected. Guest orders are linked to an anonymous session identifier, not to a Google account.

Q-Delivery uses Firebase Cloud Messaging (a Google service) to send you order-status and delivery notifications. This requires a device notification token, which is stored so notifications can be delivered to your device. You can disable notifications at any time from your device settings.

The app loads some code libraries and images (for example map and database libraries and menu or banner pictures) from third-party content servers, which can see your IP address and device information in the normal way when your device requests those files. Q-Delivery does not use advertising or analytics services and does not show ads.

Q-Delivery is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so it can be removed.

10. Data Security

Q-Delivery takes reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect customer information against unauthorized access, misuse or loss.

However, no internet service or electronic storage system can guarantee absolute security.

11. Account & Data Deletion

Customers who signed in with Google can delete their Q-Delivery account at any time from the Delete Account option in the app's Settings. Guest sessions have no account to delete. You can also request deletion of your account and personal data by emailing qdeliverymalakand@gmail.com.

Account deletion may permanently remove access to the account and may result in the deletion of associated personal account information, subject to information that may need to be retained for legitimate business, legal or security purposes.

12. Your Choices

  • You can choose whether to provide device location permission.
  • You can enter your delivery address manually instead of using device location.
  • Your location is read only when you tap “Use Current Location”. You can instead drag the map pin or type your delivery address yourself.
  • You can change your selected district at any time by tapping the district name at the top of the home screen.
  • You can request deletion of your customer account.
  • You can clear a locally stored promo code at any time by clearing your browser/app storage, or it will be replaced automatically the next time you activate a different one.
  • You can contact Q-Delivery support regarding privacy or personal information questions.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Q-Delivery may update this Privacy Policy when the service, features or data practices change.

The latest version will be made available in the Q-Delivery customer app.

14. Contact

For privacy questions, data deletion requests or concerns about your personal information, contact Q-Delivery customer support.

Customer Support Email: qdeliverymalakand@gmail.com

15. Mystery Box

What it is: A Mystery Box is a surprise food order. The restaurant selects and packs the items. You choose the box and, where available, your preferred food type, but you do not choose the specific dishes.

Contents vary: The contents may differ from one order to the next, even when you order the same box from the same restaurant. Items are selected from the restaurant's available menu on the day.

Value: Each Mystery Box is intended to contain food with a total menu value higher than the price paid. The estimated value shown in the app is a guide only.

Items shown in the app: Any items displayed in the app after delivery are for information and entertainment. The food you receive is what the restaurant has packed, and it may differ from what the app displays.

Allergies and dietary needs: Because you do not choose the dishes, please state any allergies or dietary requirements in the order note before you place the order, and check the packaging and ingredients before eating. Q-Delivery and the restaurant cannot guarantee that a Mystery Box is free of any particular allergen unless the restaurant has confirmed this to you.

Cancellations and refunds: A Mystery Box cannot be cancelled once the restaurant has accepted the order. Refunds are not available because you dislike an item, since the contents are a surprise. If your order is incorrect, damaged, has missing items or is unsafe to eat, please contact customer support as soon as possible and your request will be reviewed.

Availability: Mystery Boxes are offered in limited quantities and may be unavailable at any time.
